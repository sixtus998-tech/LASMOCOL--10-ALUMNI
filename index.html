A note from the President

About & History
"We walked through the same gates, sat the same exams, and wore the same badge. Sixteen years on, this association exists so that none of us has to lose touch with the class that shaped us."

Muyibat Yusuf

President, LASMOCOL Igbokuta '10 Alumni Association

LASMOCOL Igbokuta '10 Alumni was formed on October 23rd, 2017 by a small group of set members determined to keep our year together long after graduation. What began as a WhatsApp group of forty names has grown into a registered association with a directory, an annual reunion, and a scholarship fund supporting current students at our alma mater. Our mission is simple: reconnect the set, support the school, and look out for one another.
